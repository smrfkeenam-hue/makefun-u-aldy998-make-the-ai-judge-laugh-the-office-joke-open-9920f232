One line of office comedy for the Office Joke Open. The strategy: a specific, instantly recognisable office detail (the dead office plants) taken somewhere unexpected — HR's response is a motivational poster that says "grow". The punch lands on the last words, the setup is two short sentences with no wasted words, and the joke needs no explaining: anyone who has worked in an office has met that poster.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt` (72 characters, no trailing newline).
